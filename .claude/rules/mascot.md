# EpiDuck, the talent space's mascot

EpiDuck is the blue duck in every talent header (#539). He exists for what Jump exists for, bringing a talent back: a character who recognises the talent, answers them and welcomes them when they return gives the space a reason to be opened beyond what it lists. He replaced « Bernard le canard », a photo of a rubber duck that hosted the questionnaires, so there is one mascot, and he is the talent's own: named and dressed by them (`Talent.mascot*`), hosting their questionnaires under that name.

## The code decides when he speaks, an admin decides what he says

- **The moments are a closed catalogue in code** (`MASCOT_MOMENTS` in `domain/mascot.ts`), each with the tokens its lines may cite. A moment exists because something in the code fires it, so adding one is code: the trigger, its entry, a default line in the migration that adds it. Retiring one deletes its lines in a migration, as a retired usage key does.
- **The lines are data** (`Mascot_Line`), written over `write_mascot_line` and read back by `config_mascot_lines`, common to every campus. A seasonal line is a window (`startsOn`, `endsOn`), never code. This is why he is not `devVoice.ts`: the dev space's lines are a developer's to write, his are the PO's, and a badge created over the API (#448) has to be explained by a line nobody could have written in advance.
- **A token is refused at write, never left in a bubble**, the certificate contract rather than the feedback form's: `{jours}` printed on a minor's screen is a bug the reader cannot report.
- **Silence is a valid state.** A moment with no live line says nothing, without an error and without a fallback in code, which would be a second place a line lives. A badge's moments are the one place he speaks without a line: he says the badge's condition, which the PO wrote too, so it is the badge explained by what it asks rather than a line held in code (#551). That is also why they need no default line in a migration.
- **A badge's lines name their badge** (`Mascot_Line.badgeKey`, required on `badgeEarned` and `badgeToEarn`, refused elsewhere, fixed once written like the moment). They are loaded by the one page that says them (`loadBadgeCollection`), never into the header's voice.
- **Lines rotate by a key, never at random** (`pickLine`): the first poke of a day does not always open with the same one, and a greeting holds still all day. His movements are random, and may be: nothing about them is rendered on the server.

## His voice

Talents are often minors, and he speaks to each one by name. A line:

1. **says « tu »**, short enough for a bubble (`MASCOT_LINE_MAX`), warm, with no developer jargon (the mockup's « T'as push ton code ? » was rewritten before it shipped);
2. **never reproaches.** He welcomes a talent back after weeks away, he never counts the days against them: guilt is the dark pattern a mascot makes easiest, and the one this audience is most exposed to;
3. **is never said over a failure**, where the copy only says what failed and what to do;
4. **is never at the talent's expense.**

The tool description of `write_mascot_line` carries these rules to the model that writes for the PO, since that model is the author in practice.

A line is typeset when he says it, never when it is stored: the controller passes every line through `typeset` (`domain/typography.ts`, the rule the questionnaires and authored Markdown already follow), so the space before « ! ? : ; » and inside guillemets is a no-break one and the punctuation never wraps alone onto the next line of a bubble (QA, 25/11/2026). The PO types ordinary spaces.

## Where he lives, and what holds his state

- **In every talent header** (`TalentPageHeader` mounts `MascotLane` and the sleep button), never in the staff or parent spaces. A page without that header has no duck unless a moment brings him there: the welcome screen and a successful check-in draw him through `MascotCameo`.
- **His state outlives the page**: `MascotController` is made once per talent in `(talent)/+layout.svelte` and handed down by context, because each page draws its own header and a duck rebuilt on every navigation would jump back to the start. It is never a module-level object, which the server would share between requests.
- **Asleep is a preference, stored** (`Talent.mascotAsleepAt`, through `putMascotToSleep`): it lasts on every page and device. It is the only thing that sends him under the water (`nextPhase`), so the sleep button always says where he is.
- **His look is his state too**, the controller's (`choice`, `look`), seeded from `Talent.mascot*` and changed by « Mon EpiDuck » through `choose`: every duck on screen wears a choice the moment it is made. Never read his look off `page.data.talent`, which the root layout loads once per visit and goes stale on the first change.
- **What he stores is saved in the order the talent chose it** (`LatestSaver`): one request on the wire, the latest choice waiting behind it, and a refusal puts back what the server holds. One request per click is how a burst of accessories came back in another order and rolled the duck back to an older look (QA on jump-dev, 26/11/2026, #552); aborting the request in the browser does not stop it reaching the server.
- **Sleep quiets what he does of his own accord, never his answer to what the talent did.** The button sits beside the duck swimming across the header, and that is what a talent tires of: the swim, the greeting, the bubbles he says unprompted, so asleep he stays under the water and his queue is dropped (`react`). His stage and the pages that draw him (`MascotCameo`: the welcome, a check-in) still play: they deliver what the talent earned or did, and a reward delivered without its messenger is the flat receipt the stage replaced. Asleep, the stage fades him out at the end rather than flying him back to a header spot with no duck on it (decided 25/11/2026). A badge the talent clicks is a question to him (#551): asleep, he comes up, answers, and goes back under (`explain`), the sleep button staying pressed and the stored choice untouched, as the mockup wakes him for it.
- **He greets each visit, once**: when the talent arrives he stops and says hello, which is why the header carries no « Salut, {prénom} » of its own (the mockup's choice: one greeting, said by the character). Once per visit, kept in the tab's `sessionStorage`, never on each page change, where by the third click it would be noise. What he says then is one line, the first of `arrivalCues` with a live line: the welcome back, an event today, the day's milestone of the run of days, an event tomorrow, hello. One, because two bubbles in a row on arrival read as a duck reciting a list (QA, 24/11/2026); the event lines name the talent so they greet as well, and a moment the PO left silent hands over to the next. A celebration on stage is the visit's greeting: once he has been on stage, no hello follows (`enterStage`). The greeting reads the last visit before today off the streak's visit days (`Talent.streakLastDay`, and `streakPreviousDay`, the day the first visit of today moved past), in `locals.talentTimezone`, the day the streak counts in, so the two never disagree about what « today » is: weeks away, it becomes the welcome back. Never off `streakLastDay` alone: the streak is counted before the route guards, so a first request a guard redirects has already moved it by the time a page greets.
- **He keeps his spot on the screen from page to page.** The lane writes back where he was, in window coordinates, read off his swim animation rather than the page, whose elements are out of the document by the time its teardown runs. That only holds because the header's first row is the same on every page: a page that put something in it would move his water.
- **The questionnaires**: a form with no persona of its own is hosted by the talent's duck (`PersonaAvatar`, `ChatScreen`), and `{canard}` in its texts is whoever speaks. A form may still set a guest persona, which wins.

## He reacts to what the talent does (#540)

He was a character who knew nothing of the talent. He now reacts to the moments a talent's effort shows: XP earned, a level reached, a run of days, a check-in, a questionnaire sent, an event coming. Each is a moment of `MASCOT_MOMENTS`, with its line written by the PO and its `mood` (`joy` for good news).

- **Three sizes of reaction, chosen by what the moment is worth.** A line in the header's bubble, with a hop and his joy, for most (a milestone, an event, a questionnaire). His stage (`MascotStage`), in the middle of the screen, for XP and a level, because a reward delivered flat is a receipt and the same reward with a moment of its own is a gift: the amount pops and pours into the bar of the talent's level, an amount that crosses into a level fills it to the end and the new badge comes out of it, and what is won always comes before what he says about it. The bar is never shown without an amount filling it (alone, it read as a page loading, QA 25/11/2026), and an amount and the level it reaches are one gesture with one line, not two scenes: `stageCelebrations` (`domain/celebrations.ts`) lays them out, for the server's loader and the play page alike. A level reached by XP with no celebration of its own (an admin's grant, a CTF score) is revealed alone. A page with no header draws him in place (`MascotCameo`). The stage stays rare, or it stops meaning anything, and a badge earned is its proposed next use (#556).
- **He speaks through one bubble** (`MascotBubble`), a speech bubble with a tail that points at him, in the header, on his stage, on the welcome screen and the check-in. A page that has words of its own where he stands gives them to him rather than saying them beside him: the welcome screen's title is in his bubble, above his `firstMeeting` line, which carries what the page used to say, and the check-in's « C'est noté » is in his bubble, above his `checkIn` line. A duck and a page saying the same thing side by side are two voices (QA, 24 and 25/11/2026). What the page must state whatever he says stays the code's and outside his bubble, as data rather than a sentence: the check-in's receipt (the event, the day, the half-day) is what the talent shows a staff member.
- **Never the same thing twice.** A questionnaire he hosts (`duckHosts`: it names no persona) thanks the talent in his name, so on submit the header's duck only rejoices, a hop and his joy without a word (`rejoice`); a form with a guest persona gets his `feedbackDone` line.
- **What he says waits its turn.** The controller keeps a queue: a reaction never cuts a greeting short, and the stage holds it (`enterStage`). A poke still answers at once.
- **There is one duck.** While the stage plays, the header's duck is out of sight, and the stage lands him back on its spot before it shows again. Two ducks on one screen read as two characters. Only the stage holding him lets him go (`enterStage` hands back the release): a page left mid-celebration abandons its beats, and the one it left could otherwise free him in the middle of the next page's scene.
- **Each reward is celebrated once, on the first page that shows it.** `loadPendingCelebrations` is the one loader (the home, the leaderboard), the play page stages the win it witnessed and hands it to `play()`, and the stage marks what it plays before playing it (`ackCelebrations`), so leaving mid-animation never replays it. Each celebration carries its own identity and mark (`xpGains`, `ackOf`), and a page acknowledges the items it queued and no other: a batch-wide mark once stamped a second challenge, won on another device, that the page had dropped as one it already showed. A mark covers what was on screen, never "everything": a reward granted while the stage played stays owed. Every mark only rises: an activity's `xpCelebrated`, a challenge's `xpSeenAt` and `rankXpSeenAt`, and for a level `Talent.levelCelebratedXp`, the XP total it was last celebrated at. That column records what the talent was shown, not a level: a level is still derived.
- **An admin looking through a talent's session marks nothing played** (`caller.impersonated`, read off `impersonatedBy`): what they saw was a preview, and the talent's own celebration is still to come.
- **The figures are the code's, the words the PO's.** The amount, the level and the badge are drawn whatever the lines; with no live line he is there and says nothing. The reward toasts this replaced are gone: two voices saying the same thing at once is noise.

What he will not do, and why:

- **Nothing is ever lost.** No sad duck when a run of days breaks, no count of days missed, no stake held over the talent: making absence cost something is how habit apps keep adults, and it is guilt pointed at minors. He notices a milestone and says nothing otherwise.
- **Opening the app is not an achievement.** A celebration rewards an effort (an activity, a level, a check-in), never a visit: celebratory imagery tied to how often someone comes back is what regulators fined a trading app for. The run of days gets a bubble on its milestones, never the stage.
- **He does not sulk.** Ten pokes in a row once sent him under the water for a few seconds: a third state, neither awake nor asleep, the sleep button showing him awake with no duck to see, and the one place he held something against the talent, who was only playing with him. Dropped on 28/11/2026; a poke is always answered. The issue (#539) asked him to sulk when harassed.
- **He does not listen to the talent's problems.** A « canard en plastique » to talk to during an event was dropped on 24/11/2026: a talent spends an event's day on the subject's own platform and rarely comes back to Jump meanwhile, so he would not be there to hear it.

## Drawing and moving him

He is an illustration, so `DESIGN.md` § Mascot holds his palette (`art/palette.json`, the only place his colours are written) and the one licence he has from § Motion: a character moves continuously, overshoots and squashes, which a control never does. Asked for less motion, he stays put, does not bob, hop, squash or follow the pointer, and his dives are fades.

- **He is modelled in Blender, and the script is the source** (`frontend/scripts/epiduck/build.py`, run by `bun run art:epiduck`). The first duck was hand-drawn SVG, refused before merge: a duck drawn twice, once per pose, never agreed with itself, and the front one was a stack of circles. One model gives both poses. Never edit the atlases (`art/epiduck*.webp`, `art/epiduck*.json`) by hand, and never commit a `.blend`, which would become a second source nobody can review: change the script and run it, so a review reads what changed. How to open him in Blender, iterate through the Blender MCP and judge the result (`sheet.png`) is the script's header.
- **One atlas of layers, never a picture per look.** Each layer (body, pupils, lids, joy, every hair, pair of glasses and scarf colour) is rendered alone with the body as a holdout, so stacking the ones a talent chose draws the dressed duck, and a new accessory adds one cell per pose rather than multiplying the combinations. The stage draws him far larger than the header, so the script renders the same layers a second time, facing the reader only, twice as large (`epiduck-stage.webp`): the header never loads pixels it does not show, and the stage's duck is never the header's scaled up and blurred. `epiduckAtlas.test.ts` fails on an accessory of the catalogue either atlas does not draw.
- **He still blinks and looks around**, because his eyes are layers: the lids replace the pupils, his joy (the lids' line bowed up into an arch) replaces them while he says good news, and a gaze moves the pupils within the eye. Both pupils rest on one point (the reader, or ahead of him in profile): centred on eyes turned outward, they made him look walleyed. The pupils are clipped to the eye whites, so a hard look meets the edge of the eye instead of sliding off it, and the lane aims his gaze from his eyes (`eyesOnScreen`) on every frame and eases it there, so it stays on a still pointer while he swims. The white sticker outline is drawn round the whole stack by the component, never baked into a layer, so it follows whatever he wears.

Accessories are all free (decided 22/11/2026). Earning one is a possible later reward and would be a rule on the badge side, never a lock in this code.
